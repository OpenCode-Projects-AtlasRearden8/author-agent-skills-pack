# Workflow Notes for the Series Fact-Sheet Keeper

When the skill runs:

1. Find `canon/factsheet.md`; create from `templates/series-factsheet.md` (pack root) if missing, seeded only from files the author names.
2. Read the material the author designates (new chapters, outline, published text).
3. Produce two lists: **new facts** and **possible conflicts** — with book + chapter citations for every item.
4. Present a proposed diff. Wait. Factsheet edits change canon; the author approves.
5. After approval, apply the diff, keeping `~corrected`/`~draft` history on updated lines, and bump the "updated" date in the header.
6. Canon briefing (on request): filter facts relevant to named characters/places and print them as a plain list.

Prohibitions: no inventing canon, no deleting confirmed facts, no unstated merges of two entries, no pushing facts to any external service.
